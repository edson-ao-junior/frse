## Featured research

**FREM: Forensic Readiness in Software Engineering.** The research program's initial evidence-based conceptual model was derived from a completed multivocal synthesis. The systematic mapping included **99 academic studies**, while the grey-literature review accepted **54 sources**. The synthesis and the related model documentation are being prepared for staged public release.

[Read about FREM](research-outputs/frem.md) · [Study S01](studies/s01-multivocal-review/) · [Current status](progress/status.md)
