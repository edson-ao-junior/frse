# FRSE-S01-ONT · Forensic Readiness Ontology

**Research status:** Planned extension.  
**WP:** [WP1](../../work-packages/wp1-foundations.md)

## Purpose

Investigate whether the FREM conceptual model and taxonomy can support a formal, machine-interpretable ontology of forensic-readiness concepts, relationships and constraints.

## Potential research steps

1. Identify the scope and competency questions.
2. Reuse validated FREM and taxonomy definitions as inputs.
3. Model concepts, relations and constraints using suitable ontology languages.
4. Check consistency and alignment with competency questions.
5. Conduct expert or use-case-based evaluation.

No ontology artifact has been claimed as completed or publicly released here.
