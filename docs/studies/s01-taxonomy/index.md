# FRSE-S01-TAX · FREM Taxonomy

**Working title:** A Taxonomy of Forensic Readiness in Software Engineering  
**Research status:** Development in progress; independent evaluation and final study results are pending.  
**WP:** [WP1](../../work-packages/wp1-foundations.md)

## Purpose

Develop a systematic classification of forensic-readiness constructs and their characteristics from the evidence-derived FREM conceptual foundation.

## Planned reporting

The work should explain the taxonomy design method, dimensional structure, classification decisions, relationships among constructs and evaluation plan. The article will distinguish design outcomes from evidence generated through independent evaluation.

## Deliverables

- Taxonomy dimensions and characteristics.
- Traceable mappings from FREM constructs to taxonomy elements.
- Classification figures and structured taxonomy tables.
- Evaluation instruments, anonymized data where suitable, and analysis.
- Scientific manuscript and accompanying public release package.

**Note:** This page documents current development, not completed independent evaluation.
