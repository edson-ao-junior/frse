# Open Science and Reproducibility

FRSE adopts transparency, traceability, inspectability and careful reuse as guiding practices for research outputs.

## Core commitments

1. Preserve study-specific protocols, eligibility rules, data extraction procedures and deviations.
2. Maintain traceability between source claims, synthesized concepts and model elements.
3. Report available evidence separately from interpretation and untested hypotheses.
4. Publish eligible artifacts and replication instructions when permissions and study conditions permit.
5. Identify restrictions, unavailable materials and the reasons for any non-release.
6. Cite source research accurately and version public releases.

**Open-science transparency does not override copyright, data protection, human-participant safeguards, blind review, or embargo requirements.**
