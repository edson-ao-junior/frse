# FREM · Forensic Readiness Conceptual Model

**FREM** is the evidence-derived conceptual foundation at the center of the FRSE research program.

## Role of the model

FREM brings together concepts and relationships relevant to forensic readiness in software engineering. Its derivation draws on the completed multivocal study, including academic evidence and grey-literature claims. The model emphasizes transparent derivation, traceability and rationale rather than a black-box readiness score.

## Evidence base

- Systematic mapping: **99 included academic studies**.
- Grey-literature review: **54 accepted sources**, **1,054 source-level claims**, **312 canonical concepts**, and **20 harmonized core constructs**.
- Multivocal integration: provenance-aware synthesis at concept level.

These evidence-base counts should not be interpreted as a definitive count of FREM model entities or as an independent validation of the model.

## Intended public artifacts

- Versioned conceptual diagram.
- Construct and relationship definitions.
- Derivation and traceability matrix.
- Model limitations and scope notes.
- Protocol and appropriate replication documentation.
