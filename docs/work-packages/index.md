# Work Packages

FRSE organizes its long-term research scope into four complementary work packages (WPs). They provide continuity even as individual studies, papers and datasets are completed.

| Package | Purpose | Initial emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [WP1](wp1-foundations.md) | Foundations and conceptualization | Multivocal evidence, FREM model, taxonomy, ontology |
| [WP2](wp2-characterization.md) | Empirical characterization | Software mining and change analysis |
| [WP3](wp3-assessment.md) | Assessment and prediction | Candidate measures and predictive research |
| [WP4](wp4-improvement.md) | Improvement and validation | Evidence gaps, actionable practices and evaluations |

WP3 and WP4 are **planned research directions**, not completed deliverables.
