# WP4 · Improvement and Validation

**Status: Planned direction.**

**Objective:** Explore techniques and interventions that improve the generation, accessibility and trustworthiness of investigative evidence in software systems.

## Proposed topics

- Identification and prioritization of forensic evidence gaps.
- Engineering practices that address traceability, rationale and provenance weaknesses.
- Case studies or controlled evaluations of proposed interventions.
- Replication and independent assessments where feasible.

Evaluation designs and outcomes will be documented in dedicated protocols and artifacts before research claims are published.
