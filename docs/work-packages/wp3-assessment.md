# WP3 · Assessment and Prediction

**Status: Planned direction.**

**Objective:** Explore how readiness could be assessed, interpreted and, where scientifically defensible, predicted from engineering evidence.

## Proposed topics

- Indicators and assessment criteria grounded in FREM constructs.
- Validity, reliability, and coverage of candidate readiness measures.
- Explainable predictive signals from software and evolution data.
- Uncertainty and conditions under which assessment results are not meaningful.

No validated assessment instrument or predictive model is claimed at this stage.
